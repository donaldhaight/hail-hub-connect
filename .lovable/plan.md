# Standing Orders — Mission Headquarters

Holding position. No build work until the refresher list and the 11-1 outline have landed.

## Frame adopted

This project is mission headquarters, not a website. We are co-founders wearing every hat: pitch men, CEO, CFO, CTO, dev team, testers, administrators.

The pattern-match is Executive Order 14363 (Genesis Mission, 24 Nov 2025): the federal answer to coordination, provenance, and trusted-decision speed in science. Our civilian counterpart is an alternative way of doing business and government that still works during an extended blackout — human coordination first, silicon second. Insurance restoration is the proof of concept because it is the same disease at survivable scale.

## Operating model — permanent

Funding is approved against documentation, not vision. The load-bearing artifact is the **Completed Project Offer**:

- PRD, scope, requirements
- Human hours and agent hours, per Project and per Sprint
- Materials, Labor, Turnkey Bid, Equipment, Other Expenses

**The rule:** if you can bill it, you can build it. Within 10% variance between estimate and actual, the project keeps running.

Consequences, in force from now:

1. Every sprint I propose arrives priced — hours and cost lines, not just scope.
2. Every completed sprint gets actuals recorded against its estimate.
3. This project is job number one. It bills itself, so the 11-1 demonstration has real historical variance data behind it rather than a claim.

## Stakeholder group seven

The Federal Government does not need a separate product. It needs a login: sign in, read an Offer where every number traces to a dated artifact and every claim wears its truth label, approve it, and let the system carry the accounting — obligated, drawn, delivered, variance, reconciled.
