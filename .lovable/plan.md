# Big Picture: Two Congresses, One Mission

Strategy session only. No code changes this pass. The deliverable is one document — `docs/STRATEGY.md` — that every future sprint is measured against.

## The timeline correction

Today is 25 August 2026. **11-1-2026 is 68 days away.** A 300-seat physical convening cannot be built, invited, filled, and produced in ten weeks at the standard this mission requires. The current site depicts exactly that, and the gap between depiction and capacity is the single largest credibility risk in the project right now.

Your reframe closes it. 11-1 stops being the destination and becomes the *opening*.

```text
11-01-2026   First Congress    streamed     the reveal, the announcement, the invitation
                               ↓  105 days of qualification
02-14-2027   Second Congress   convened     Super Bowl weekend, the owners meeting
                               ↓  annual
02-14-2028+  Owners Meeting    recurring    the permanent cultural date
```

This is better than a single event on both axes. Ten weeks is enough to produce a broadcast. And the thing a broadcast produces — a qualified, self-selected list — is exactly the input a real convening needs.

## The invitation ladder

Your line is the mechanic, and it should be stated on the site almost verbatim: **you need an invitation to receive the invitation.**

```text
Referral   →   Ticket to the stream   →   Invitation to the Congress   →   Seat
                    11-01-2026                                            02-14-2027
```

Four gates, each earned. The stream isn't marketing — it's the qualifying round. Watching it is itself a credential, and the people who receive a February seat are drawn from the people who held a November ticket. That makes the scarcity real rather than asserted, and it means the site's job between now and 11-1 is not to fill 300 seats. It's to issue tickets.

## What the Continental framing buys

First and Second Continental Congress is not decoration. It is a claim about *sequence*: the first body convened to state a grievance and establish that a body existed at all; the second convened to act. Adopting it commits you to something specific — November declares, February decides.

It also gives the mission a native vocabulary that costs nothing to explain to an American audience, and it makes the annual cadence legible. Super Bowl weekend as the owners meeting is the same move: borrowing an existing cultural date rather than asking anyone to remember a new one. Major League Baseball's winter meetings work precisely because nobody has to be told when they are.

One deliberate tension worth keeping: the Continental frame is heavy and historic, the Super Bowl frame is popular and commercial. Held together, they read as a movement that is serious about power and unembarrassed about audience. Held apart, either one alone reads as costume.

## How this changes the branding

Five concrete effects:

1. **PrepareAmerica is the mission, not the event.** Previously the domain and the conference were near-synonymous. Now PrepareAmerica spans the whole ladder, and the two congresses are named occasions *within* it. This makes the decade-long runway credible instead of aspirational.
2. **The United Stakeholders shield gains its proper job.** A convener's seal is exactly what a Congress requires. It belongs on the invitation, the ticket, the broadcast, and the Owner's Manual — the instruments of authority — not in the site header. Keeping it off the header keeps the mission name clean and makes the seal mean something when it appears.
3. **"Conference" is the wrong word now.** It suggests a trade show. The vocabulary becomes First Congress / Second Congress / Owners Meeting. This is a copy change across `/prepare-america` and the header, not a restructure.
4. **The Agenda series gets its anchor.** Agenda2028 is no longer an abstract four-year volume — it is what the Second Congress convenes to ratify. Agenda2024 remains the retroactive origin record. 2032 and 2036 stay declared and unwritten; their emptiness states the runway.
5. **The canon has a stage.** A streamed event is the one format where the books, songs, and eventual film are assets rather than distractions. They score the broadcast. That resolves the earlier tension cleanly: silent in public, present in the room, and now *performed* on 11-1 to an audience that already holds a ticket.

## Marketing posture, revised

Still minimal, but the deadline is now ten weeks, not fifteen months.

- No blog, no newsletter, no public content program. Unchanged.
- Growth stays referral-only. The referral loop already built becomes the ticket-issuance mechanism.
- Between now and 11-1, the public site has one job: convert a referred stranger into a ticket holder.
- Between 11-1 and 2-14, it has a different job: convert ticket holders into Congress delegates.
- Success before November is tickets issued to qualified names, not seats filled.

## What this pass does not do

The one thing worth flagging: the vocabulary change and the date correction are **time-sensitive**. Every day the public page depicts a 300-person physical convening on 11-1 is a day the site is describing something that won't happen as described.
