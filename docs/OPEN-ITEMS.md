SiteBMS → JobNimbus mapping → API/MCP.** The concepts opened on 2026-09-09 attach to it
as follows, so they are seen coming rather than discovered mid-sprint:

| Concept | Position |
|---|---|

ISR onboarding, the ISR→LC connection, lead origination and assignment, and offer
completion all run through the Records layer. None of them is unblocked by naming these
concepts; naming them ensures the object model is cut once, with the game, the program,
the referral position, and the consumer surface already in view.
