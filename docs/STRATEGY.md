# PrepareAmerica — Strategy Memo

**United Stakeholders of America LLC**
Written 25 August 2026. Supersedes prior assumptions about the 11-1-2026 convening.

This document is the reference every future sprint is measured against. It is not a
requirements doc and it is not a plan. It states what the mission is, what the named
occasions are for, what words we use, and what we deliberately refuse to do.

Truth class: **DECISION** unless a section states otherwise.
Confidentiality: **C1** — internal, shareable with qualified insiders.

---

## 1. Timeline

### The correction

The project was built around a single destination: 300 people, physically convened at
Gratitude Ranch in Flower Mound, Texas, on 1 November 2026.

As of this writing that date is **68 days away**. A convening of that size and standard
cannot be built, invited, qualified, filled, and produced in ten weeks. Continuing to
depict it as such is the largest credibility risk in the project — not because the
ambition is wrong, but because the depiction and the capacity have come apart.

The correction is not to move the date. It is to change what the date *is*.

### The ladder

```text
08-25-2026   Today                          68 days to the First Congress
                    ↓
11-01-2026   First Congress    streamed     the reveal, the announcement, the invitation
                    ↓          105 days of qualification
02-14-2027   Second Congress   convened     Super Bowl weekend, the owners meeting
                    ↓          annual thereafter
02-14-2028+  Owners Meeting    recurring    the permanent cultural date
```

11-1 stops being the destination and becomes the opening. This is stronger on both
axes: ten weeks is enough time to produce a broadcast, and the thing a broadcast
produces — a qualified, self-selected list of names — is precisely the input a real
convening requires.

### What must exist by 11-1-2026

- The public front door, in its current form, with corrected vocabulary.
- A ticket mechanism: referred names can be issued a credential to watch.
- The Owner's Manual, complete enough to be the artifact the broadcast points at.
- The broadcast itself: the declaration, the proof, and the invitation to February.

### What can wait for 02-14-2027

- Physical venue logistics, seat maps, hotel blocks, catering, itinerary.
- Delegate credentialing and on-site identity.
- Sponsorship instruments (PSL tiers) and anything transactional.
- The Agenda2028 ratification text in final form.

## 2. The two congresses

### First Congress — 1 November 2026, streamed

The First Continental Congress convened to state a grievance and, more importantly, to
establish that a body existed at all. It did not legislate. It declared itself.

Our First Congress does the same three things and no more:

1. **Reveal.** The seven verticals, the architecture, the twenty-five years behind it.
2. **Announce.** That United Stakeholders of America exists and has convened.
3. **Invite.** To the Second Congress, on terms.

It is streamed, not gathered. Ticketed, not open. The audience is small on purpose and
qualified on purpose.

### Second Congress — 14 February 2027, convened

The Second Continental Congress acted. It raised an army, issued currency, and adopted
a declaration.

Our Second Congress convenes physically, at 300, and is where Agenda2028 is ratified
and the operating structure is adopted. Every person in the room came through the
November broadcast.

**November declares. February decides.** That sequence is a commitment, not a slogan;
nothing that belongs to February should be attempted in November, and nothing declared
in November should go unanswered in February.

### Why Super Bowl weekend

Borrowing an existing cultural date costs nothing and buys permanent legibility. Major
League Baseball's winter meetings work because nobody has to be told when they are. The
same logic applies here: an annual owners meeting anchored to the single most reliably
observed weekend in American life does not require a marketing budget to be remembered.

### The productive tension

The Continental frame is heavy, historic, and civic. The Super Bowl frame is popular,
commercial, and unembarrassed. Held together they read as a movement that is serious
about power and comfortable with an audience. Held apart, either one alone reads as
costume. Keep both. Never soften one to match the other.

## 3. The invitation ladder

The governing mechanic, stated plainly and usable as public copy:

> You need an invitation to receive the invitation.

```text
Referral   →   Ticket to the stream   →   Invitation to the Congress   →   Seat
   ↑                11-01-2026                                          02-14-2027
insider                                        convener                  convener
referral                                       reviews                   confirms
```

| Gate | Who controls it | What it grants |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| Referral | A qualified insider (1–3 per insider) | The right to apply |
| Ticket | The convener, reviewing applications | Access to the 11-1 broadcast |
| Invitation | The convener, drawn from ticket holders | The right to a February seat |
| Seat | The convener, capped at 300 | Delegate status at the Second Congress |

Three consequences worth stating explicitly:

- **The stream is the qualifying round, not marketing.** Having watched it is itself a
  credential. This is what makes the scarcity real rather than asserted.
- **The site's job before 11-1 is to issue tickets, not fill seats.** Success is
  measured in qualified names holding credentials, never in volume.
- **February seats are drawn from November ticket holders.** No side doors. A person
  who did not hold a ticket does not receive an invitation, regardless of standing.

## 4. Brand architecture

Four layers, one door.

```text
PrepareAmerica            the mission        prepareamerica.com
  United Stakeholders     the convener       the shield, "We The People Make The Rules"
    Agenda 20XX           the record         four-year volumes
      ClaimStore / RRCA   the proof          seven verticals, held one layer in
```

**PrepareAmerica** is the mission and the front door. It spans the entire ladder and the
entire decade. It is not the name of an event. This distinction is what makes the
ten-year runway credible rather than aspirational.

**United Stakeholders of America LLC** is the convener — the body that calls a Congress
into session. It appears where authority is being exercised: invitations, tickets, the
broadcast, the Owner's Manual, signed instruments.

**Agenda 20XX** is the record: what was argued, in which cycle.

**ClaimStore, RRCA, and the seven verticals** are the proof. They stay one layer in,
behind the Three-Layer Reveal discipline (Vertical → Brand → Domain) already enforced in
the codebase. Substance precedes branding; branding precedes real estate.

### Naming rules

| Name | May appear | Must not appear |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| PrepareAmerica | Header, titles, domain, all public surfaces | — |
| United Stakeholders of America LLC | Convener line, footer attribution, instruments, manual | Site header wordmark |
| Agenda 20XX | Manual volumes, Congress materials | Public navigation (until a volume exists) |
| Brand names (seven verticals) | Behind auth only | Any signed-out surface |
| Domains (seven verticals) | Behind auth only | Any signed-out surface |

## 5. Mark usage

The United Stakeholders shield — blue and black, "WE THE PEOPLE MAKE THE RULES,"
EST. 2022 — is a **convener's seal**, not a logo.

A Congress requires a seal. That is exactly the shield's job, and it is the reason it
must not become site furniture. A mark that appears everywhere authorizes nothing.

**The shield appears on:**

- The Owner's Manual cover and title page.
- Tickets to the First Congress.
- Invitations to the Second Congress.
- Broadcast identity: opening and closing frames.
- Signed instruments and private documents.
- Delegate credentials.

**The shield does not appear on:**

- The site header. The wordmark stays typographic.
- Public marketing surfaces of any kind.
- Favicons, avatars, or social profile images used casually.
- Anything repeated often enough to become decoration.

Rule of thumb: if the surface is not conferring something on someone, the seal does not
belong on it.

## 6. Vocabulary

Language is load-bearing here in the same way truth labels are. "Conference" describes a
trade show; we are not running one.

**In use:**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Congress | A convened session. First (streamed), Second (physical). |
| Owners Meeting | The recurring annual session, from 02-14-2028 forward. |
| Convener | United Stakeholders of America LLC. |
| Delegate | A person seated at a Congress. |
| Ticket | The credential granting access to the broadcast. |
| Invitation | The instrument granting the right to a seat. |
| Seal | The United Stakeholders shield. |
| Insider | A qualified reader of the private corpus. |

**Retired:**

- conference → Congress
- attendee → delegate
- registration → application, then invitation
- signup / RSVP → never used
- event → occasion, or the specific Congress by name

## 7. The Agenda series

Four domains, four volumes, tied to US election cycles. This converts a decade of
intent into a publishing structure rather than a standing promise.

| Volume | Status | Role |
|--------|--------|------|
| Agenda2024 | Retroactive | The origin record. Written after the fact, arguing what was already true. Its rhetorical value is that it is *not* a prediction. |
| Agenda2028 | Active | What the Second Congress convenes to ratify on 02-14-2027. |
| Agenda2032 | Declared, unwritten | Runway. |
| Agenda2036 | Declared, unwritten | Runway. |

The unwritten volumes are not placeholders. Their emptiness is the statement: the
mission has a declared horizon, and the horizon is longer than any one cycle.

**Domain policy.** All four stay parked. No microsites, no separate builds, no
redirects that fragment the front door. Each becomes a volume inside the Owner's Manual
first. A volume earns its own surface only if the Congress that ratifies it says so.

## 8. Canon placement

Books, albums, and the eventual film are not a media arm. They are dated evidence of a
single unbroken thesis, and their value is entirely a function of *when* the reader
encounters them.

**The rule: silent in public, present in the room, performed on 11-1.**

- **Public surfaces: nothing.** A stranger who encounters a music video before the RRCA
  proof of concept files this as a personality project, not an infrastructure play.
  That impression is not recoverable.
- **Behind auth: the full archive.** Chronological, so the 2008–2014 material, the
  songs, and today's requirements documents sit on one timeline. A C-level who reads
  the proof and then finds the archive concludes: *this person has been building this
  since before it was fundable.* That conclusion is the entire point.
- **The First Congress broadcast: performed.** A streamed event is the one format where
  the canon is an asset rather than a distraction. The songs score the broadcast. The
  books supply its argument. The audience already holds a ticket, so context is already
  established.

**Truth labels apply to the canon as to everything else.** A song is an ASSERTION. A
2012 requirements doc is a FACT about what was specified, not a FACT about what is true
now. A chapter projecting 2032 is a HYPOTHESIS. Nothing in the archive is exempt.

## 9. Marketing posture

Fifteen months of quiet was the earlier assumption. It is now ten weeks, and the
posture tightens rather than loosens.

**Doctrine: silence is a strategy, not an absence of one.** Scarcity only reads as
scarcity if the room is genuinely hard to enter. Every public artifact makes the
private room cheaper.

**Forbidden before 11-1-2026:**

- Blog, newsletter, or any recurring public publication.
- Social media cadence of any kind.
- SEO content programs, keyword pages, or programmatic content.
- Paid acquisition.
- Press outreach.
- Any public exposure of the seven brand names or their domains.

**Permitted:**

- Referral-only growth through the existing insider referral loop.
- Direct, personal outreach by the convener.
- The existing public pages, with corrected vocabulary.

**The two conversion jobs:**

| Period | The site's single job | Success metric |
|--------|----------------------|----------------|
| Now → 11-01-2026 | Convert a referred stranger into a ticket holder | Qualified names holding tickets |
| 11-01 → 02-14-2027 | Convert ticket holders into seated delegates | Delegates confirmed, quality over count |

Never volume. Never reach. Never impressions.

## 10. Open questions

Deliberately unresolved. Kept visible so they do not resolve themselves by default.

1. **Does Gratitude Ranch remain the February venue at 300?** Flower Mound on Super Bowl
   weekend has travel and lodging implications that have not been tested.
2. **What does the broadcast actually require?** Production, platform, run of show,
   and who appears on camera besides the convener. This is the critical path to 11-1
   and is currently unspecified.
3. **How many tickets?** A stream has no physical cap, which means the cap is a
   deliberate choice. Too many and the credential is worthless; too few and February
   has no pool to draw from.
4. **Is Agenda2024 written before or after the First Congress?** Writing it first gives
   the broadcast a document to point at. Writing it after lets the Congress shape it.
5. **What is said publicly on 11-2?** The morning after a streamed reveal is the first
   real test of the silence doctrine.
6. **Do the seven brand names surface at the First Congress?** The Three-Layer Reveal
   discipline says Vertical → Brand → Domain. A broadcast to ticket holders is arguably
   inside the room. This has not been decided.
7. **Sponsorship instruments (PSL tiers).** Explicitly out of scope for the First
   Congress. Unresolved for the Second.
