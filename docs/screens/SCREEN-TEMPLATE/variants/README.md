# Variants — the Perception Library for this screen

One file per sibling: `<mood>-<audience>.md`. None overwrites another. None is "the real
one" — the Pattern is.

Each file opens with:

```yaml
---
audience: property owner | ISR | licensed contractor | counsel | capital | government | founder
persona: kimosabe | buddy-claim | prepareamerica
moment: first arrival | mid-task | after a loss | post-storm | review | closeout
mood: plain | inviting | institutional | provocative | humorous | dramatic
purpose: orient | instruct | persuade | reassure | warn | record
intensity: 1            # 1 understated … 5 loud
truth: ASSERTION
class: C0
---
```

Rules that bite at selection, not at authoring: a variant is only servable into a surface at
or above its class; band 3 is unreachable at any mood or intensity; a dramatized
`HYPOTHESIS` is still presented as a hypothesis; and which variant was served, to whom, why
and when is recorded.

Two questions are open and bound this folder until they are answered: how far the "never
performs personality" red line moves (C37), and which adaptation signals may persist versus
stay session-local (C38).
