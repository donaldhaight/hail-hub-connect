## What the founder said

The founder described three parallel systems and asked that the project carry all of them
without letting one quietly replace another.

The first is **this application** — Kimosabe, the Doors, App Home, the Manual, the ledger.
The founder's words: we do not need it as a product, we are learning from it; but we do need
Kimosabe and where all of this is going. `DECISION` (founder, 2026-09-30).

The second is **Circle.so** — a skeleton community build the founder has worked in for two
years. It is a learning and rehearsal environment, not required infrastructure.
`DECISION` (founder, 2026-09-30).

The third is **Siteforum / ClaimExpress** — the portal stack (community, RBAC, intranet
applications, scripting middleware, database administration, IDE) on which ClaimExpress was
built from the founder's Publisher screens, 2008–2019. The founder states it is close to
working as needed, has not been touched since 2019, is backed up on a drive in Mark
Schlenzig's office, and was run at roughly $2,500 a month when taken offline. `ASSERTION`
(founder account; none of it inspected by this project). The founder will engage the
Siteforum team separately. `DECISION`.

"We have to do both" — parallel alternative systems, dueling codebases, each learning from the
other. `DECISION`.

## The wildcard

The founder frames the triad — and the invitation to every programmer and every model to run
its own stack and plan — as the variable in the Quantum Dashboard: the multiplier, not the
product. `ASSERTION` (positioning, not a claim a sponsor can diligence yet).

## The Founding Sponsor ask

The founder seeks a set of Founding Sponsors assigning a minimum of **$10,000,000 in value**
to the start-up plan, excluding RRCA and the Gratitude Ranch, which are "in play". `DECISION`
on the target; `OPEN` on everything that would make it an offer: what "assign value" means
legally (cash, in-kind, commitment, pledge), what a sponsor receives, whether any territory or
exclusivity is implied (ADR-015 and ADR-026: geography never quietly creates exclusivity), and
how NCOI applies. Counsel owns these. Nothing here is a securities offer, and no public page
may take capital intake (ADR-028 §6 stands).
