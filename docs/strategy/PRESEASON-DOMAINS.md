# Preseason Domains — One Engine, Many Doors

*DECISION (architecture, ADR-030) · the season spine is HYPOTHESIS until each season's copy
is approved. Confidentiality: C1.*

## The doctrine

Each venture domain is its own Interest Door at its own address, served by this platform.
claimstore.com opens on ClaimStore; buddyclaim.com opens on Buddy Claim; kimosabe.ai opens
on Kimosabe. The stranger never meets PrepareAmerica unless they go looking for the movement
behind the door. To the world: separate sites, separate voices, separate promises.
Underneath: one engine, one Interested User routine, one continuing person, one ledger.

Separation of expression does not require separation of infrastructure — that was ADR-029's
lesson. The point of preseason is measurement: which message makes a stranger raise their
hand. Doors built where the Interested User routine already lives make every arrival
measurable against the same record, with no ferry between systems. And because a Door here is
a content record with a promise version, eighteen months of evolving copy is a series of
edits, not rebuilds.

Domains are connected to the project with **no primary domain set**, so each serves the
platform at its own address rather than redirecting. Paths remain shared:
claimstore.com/architecture and prepareamerica.com/architecture reach the same page, because
the architecture is shared. Only the front page wears the domain's face.

## The season spine

The messaging is expected to evolve through named seasons. Each Door's promise version is
recorded per season, so we can always say which promise a person arrived on.

| Season | Window | What the doors carry |
|---|---|---|
| Preseason | now → 2026-11-01 | Message-first preview copy. Learning, not claiming. Nothing publishes without explicit founder instruction and counsel review where required. |
| Season 1 | 2026-11-01 launch → 2027-02-14 | The announced venture set, at real domains, converting arrival into the Interested User routine. Closes at the Super Bowl Weekend Owners Meeting / Congress. |
| The turn | after 2027-02-14 | Post-Congress corrections; the message shifts from invitation to evidence of Season 1. |
| Hurricane Season 2027 / Season 2 | 2027 season | The operating message: readiness, response, the network performing in public. |

Dates beyond the 11-1-2026 launch and the 2-14-2027 Owners Meeting are planning markers,
not commitments.

## What this does not change

- The locked work order. Doors are content; nothing here models ahead of its gate.
- Interest is not authority. No door grants a role, credential, group or permission.
- The gates on copy: everything is preview copy until the founder approves it and, where
  needed, counsel reviews it. Publishing remains a founder act.
- Buddy Claim waits for the registry review — the founder's own sequencing. The first
  domain door is whichever venture the founder names.
- United Stakeholders remains the intentional eighth: a portfolio container, not a Door in
  the execution set.

## The two open obligations this raises

1. **A78 — entry-context persistence.** With real domains, browser-only arrival context
   becomes a blindfold: we cannot learn which door's message works if the context dies in
   the visitor's browser. The narrow additive proposal (entry_door, campaign, interest,
   promise_version, referral source on the existing anchor/request record) is drafted and
   awaiting authorization. Many domains are the strongest argument yet for approving it.
2. **Domain inventory.** Which domains are owned, at which registrar, expiring when —
   asserted in the seven-venture package, never documented. Founder-supplied facts, one
   register line.
